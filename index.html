A LETTER FROM MY HEART

To my Shirleen, my favourite person.

If I could keep one thing from every beautiful day, it would be the feeling of having you in my life. Somehow, you have become a part of my favourite thoughts, my quiet smiles, and the little moments that make ordinary days feel special.

Since 19 April 2026, our story has been creating memories that I never want to take for granted. And even though this little museum can keep our pictures and words, my favourite memories are still the ones we have yet to make.

I cannot promise that every day will be perfect, but I want to keep learning you, choosing you, listening to you, and showing you in the little ways that you matter to me.

So whenever you visit this little corner of the internet, remember that every picture here has a place in a story that means so much to me. And the next chapter? I hope we get to write it together, one beautiful moment at a time.

You will always have a special place in my heart. ♥

With all my love, Ngugi ♥
